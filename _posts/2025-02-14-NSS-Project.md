## Summary

According to Homer, approximately 3200 years ago (in 1180 BC), flames lit up the city of Troy,
symbolizing the win of the Greeks and the triumph of the unrivaled hero, Ulysses. With his quest
ended, little did he know that his journey hadn’t even started yet. In the following 10 years, the king
of Ithaca would encounter several obstacles while sailing through Greece’s main seas, such as angry
storms, fighting Cyclops, resisting the witch Circe’s magic as well as the sirens and many more. The
powerful determination to return home after rightfully completing his mission is what keeps
Ulysses alive in our minds as a symbol of resilience and perseverance, as well as what bounded us to
use his name for a glorious and one-of-a-kind project which will continue his legacy: The Ulysses
Space Settlement.

The general trajectory of our space settlement will be travelling from Earth to Mars and back.
During the 520 days of orbiting the earth, the Ulysses focuses on collecting and destroying space
debris in order to facilitate space travelling. Moreover, this period of time gives us the perfect
opportunity to elaborate an unique experience, the Space Hotel - a relaxing vacation for people
who want to take a break from ordinary life and take part in the voyage of a lifetime.
The other part of the route consists in exploring and exploiting the red planet, as well as
transporting workers from Earth. During this term, the settlement’s own interest is in mining and
collecting resources, although several missions can be proposed by companies which want to
conduct their own experiments by using Ulysses as means of transportation, in exchange for
money, as we are an independent organization. Since we do not have any affiliations with any
government or company, we give any establishment from around the world the chance to
accomplish their own purposes on Mars, for a well set price.
Thanks to the settlement, we offer them a way of transportation for their crew and storage space
for any gear or goods. The members of the crew will be accommodated at the Hotel and given
access to storage spaces and other facilities of Ulysses, with the purpose of making their stay as
pleasant as possible, but also help them prepare for their mission. The fee changes depending on
the asked services and on the size or number of machines and people that need to be taken care of.
This exchange will grant us high income, since Mars has a lot to offer and this service is provided
exclusively by us.
